For Law Firms

A Website That Brings In the Right Clients

Your website should make new clients feel confident before they even pick up the phone. We build sites that explain what your firm does in plain language, so the right people reach out.

  • Clear Practice Areas: Visitors can quickly find the exact kind of legal help they need.
  • Attorney Profiles: Simple, easy-to-browse pages showing each lawyer's background and experience.
  • Secure Contact Forms: A private, safe way for potential clients to reach out to you.
  • Rules-Compliant Wording: We keep your site's language within your bar association's advertising rules.

For Schools

A Website That Helps Families Choose You

Your website is often a family's very first impression of your school. We build sites that make it simple for prospective parents to learn about you, for current families to find what they need, and for your staff to keep everything up to date.

  • Simple Admissions: An easy way for families to ask questions, take a virtual tour, and see deadlines.
  • One School Calendar: All term dates, events, and updates, kept in one place.
  • Parent & Student Access: Quick, secure links to portals, daily notices, and school resources.
  • Works for Everyone: Built so families using screen readers or any device can use it comfortably.
